Dostoevsky’s *The Idiot* has a large cast with long names, and the same person is often called several different things. This interactive map brings more than twenty main characters and their relationships together in one place, to consult while you read.

**[Open the character map](/en/projects/the-idiot-characters/graph/)**

- Characters are grouped by family: the Yepanchins, the Ivolgins and the Lebedevs, with Myshkin, Nastasya Filippovna and Rogozhin in the middle.
- Relationships come in five kinds, each with its own colour and line style: family, love and marriage, conflict, friendship and help, and money and patronage. Each kind can be switched on and off.
- Hover over a character to highlight only the people connected to them; click a character or a line to read about it.
- Later plot and the ending are hidden by default and appear only when you tick “Show later plot and the ending”.
- Below the map is a list of the characters, grouped by family.

For more on the novel, the Reviews section has Hermann Hesse’s [“Thoughts on The Idiot”](/en/reviews/hesse-thoughts-on-the-idiot/).
