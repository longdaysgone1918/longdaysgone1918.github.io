I am probably the kind of person who uses childhood to heal the rest of life. Over these years, I feel I have kept losing people and things tied to my memories. Because I keep losing them for one reason or another, I sometimes wonder whether certain things or details in my memory ever really existed. Sometimes I find it hard to tell others about my past, because many of the things and people that could confirm it have disappeared (though not all of them). But sometimes, through some chance, I confirm that something tucked away in a corner of my memory was real, and that makes me happy for quite a while. I know some readers dislike the somewhat clichéd story of the madeleine in *In Search of Lost Time*, but I can say that this passage describes precisely the joy I feel when a sensory connection with the past is suddenly reestablished. Also, because I lost my account on another site I used before, something like a blog and review site, I want to take this opportunity to gradually move some fragments of the past here. Thank you for visiting!
